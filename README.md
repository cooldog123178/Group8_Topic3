# Group 8 – Topic 3: Which Bus Routes Should Have More Services at Peak Hours?

## Project Overview

This project aims to identify bus routes that experience high passenger demand and high vehicle occupancy during peak hours.

The analysis compares passenger boardings (`HourBoard`), alightings (`HourAlight`), and onboard passenger load (`HourLoad`) with available capacity (`HourCapac`). The Volume-to-Capacity (V/C) ratio is used to evaluate passenger demand relative to bus capacity across different peak timebands.

The results can help identify routes with high demand and limited capacity, providing insights into where additional bus services could be considered.

## Dataset

- **Source:** [Transport for London (TfL) – Bus Crowding Data](https://crowding.data.tfl.gov.uk/#!BUSTO%2F2025-2026%2F)
- **Dataset:** `2025_26 MAX DEMAND HOUR BY ROUTE BY TIMEBAND All Routes.csv`
- **Data period:** 2025–2026

## Objectives

- Analyze passenger demand across bus routes and peak timebands.
- Compare passenger loads with available bus capacity.
- Calculate and examine the Volume-to-Capacity (V/C) ratio.
- Identify routes that may benefit from additional services during peak hours.
